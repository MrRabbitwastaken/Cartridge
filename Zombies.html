SETTINGS

Clean hides health, points, round and weapon panels. Ammo appears when you press R. Prompts, notifications and the crosshair stay.

On: press 9 to deploy the exoskeleton instantly (testing). Off: you must earn it — 30 kills in a row without taking damage, then press 9.

Quality changes render resolution and view distance.

THE STORY. Survivors are still alive in the facility. Talking never pauses the round — zombies keep coming while you chat (the comms room is the one safe area, and only for a while). Quests can be accepted, ignored or picked up later. Three endings exist; which ones you can reach depends on everything you do, not one choice.

AFTER HOURS. The park is dark for the first rounds. From round 5 the lights and rides come back; from round 10 the coaster's ground-level crossing kills anything on it (watch the flashing signals), the log flume floods its yard and slows everyone, and the bumper-car floor electrifies in bursts. Lure zombies into all three. The RIDE CONTROL master switch in the Coaster Yard starts a power surge: it opens the sealed Big Top (where the Crucible is), brings a horde and pays double points while it lasts.

DEADWOOD. Buy dynamite at the blacksmith (up to three sticks) and plant it on boarded or cracked walls: the mine entrance, a collapsed tunnel and two hidden rooms. Rigged plungers underground collapse a tunnel — killing everything in it and stopping zombies coming through for two rounds. Ride the train from the station up to the mine (anything on the tracks gets run over). From round 6, ring the saloon bell for the SALOON SHOWDOWN. Light the three boilers in the mine to wake it up… and see where it leads.

SURVIVAL TIPS. Zombies tear the boards off the windows before climbing in, so keep them repaired. Perk machines are spread across the facility (max four perks). Brutes arrive every fifth round, runners from round 3, exploders from round 6 and armoured zombies from round 7 — aim for the head, or use the repeater and heavy MG, which punch through armour.

Survive each round by killing every zombie. Earn points for hits, kills and headshots, then spend them on doors, chalk-outline wall weapons and the Mystery Box in the Main Corridor. Buying a weapon you already own refills its ammo.
