HOW TO PLAY
The goal

Type a game title and submit a guess. Each guess reveals how close you were across Year, Publisher, Critic score, Genre, Platform, Sales, and Time to Beat — keep guessing until you land the exact game.

Reading the board
Exact match Close No match

▲ / ▼ next to a number means the answer is higher or lower than your guess. Genre and Platform show individual pills that light up per tag, so you can see exactly which ones the mystery game shares.

Clues

A Perspective clue (First-Person, Third-Person, Top-Down, etc.) unlocks after your 4th guess. A bonus fact about the game unlocks after your 9th guess.

Cover art

The blurred cover art next to the Guess button belongs to the mystery game. Every time a field on the board turns green — Year, Publisher, Critic, Genre, Platform, Sales, or Time to Beat — the cover sharpens by 20%. Genre and Platform count as soon as just one shared pill lights up green, so you don't need every tag to match. Any 5 of those 7 fields turning green is enough for a fully clear cover; solving the game (or giving up) reveals it completely either way.

Notes

Sales figures are approximate public lifetime estimates (millions of units/copies/players). Critic scores are approximate review-aggregate consensus. Time to Beat reflects a typical main-story completion estimate in the spirit of community-reported averages (not a live feed), in hours. All figures are meant for gameplay, not precise reporting.
